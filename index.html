Ride Control

Unofficial companion app for Bluetooth e-scooters. Live dashboard, rides, battery insights and a 3D model.

Unofficial app. Not affiliated with any scooter manufacturer.

Your scooter, on one screen

Connect your Bluetooth scooter and see speed, battery, voltage and riding mode live. Record your rides, learn your real range and keep everything on your phone.

Coming soon to Google Play

Live dashboard

  • Speed, charge, voltage, mileage
  • Riding mode, cruise control, Zero Start
  • Stays connected in the background

Ride tracking

  • Route on a map, distance, speeds
  • Ride history and saved rides
  • Daily and weekly goals, streaks

Battery insights

  • Estimated battery health
  • Voltage sag and learning range estimate
  • Acceleration timer 0–30 / 50 / 70

Privacy policy

Good to know

Which scooters does it work with?

Bluetooth LE G-series scooters. Verified on KuKirin G2, G3, G4 and G2 Master. Features differ by model.

Do I need an account?

No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Your data stays on your phone.

Is it official?

No. Ride Control is an independent, unofficial app and is not affiliated with any scooter manufacturer.
